How Motus works

Three steps, then you put the phone down.

  1. 1 Position the camera Prop the phone where it can see all of you. The app checks the framing and tells you what to move.
  2. 2 Follow the voice It counts your reps out loud and calls out one thing to fix when your form slips.
  3. 3 Review the set Every rep scored, the faults counted, and what to work on before the next set.
You can see this again in Settings.
