    What it measures

    Wall sit — the angle at your knee between hip and ankle, which should sit in 85–110°; the angle your shin makes with the floor, plumb at 90° and allowed 85–95°, where past 95° your heels are ahead of your knees and under 85° they are behind them; and how far the line from your hip to your shoulder leans off vertical, allowed ±12°.

    Elbow plank — where your shoulder sits over your elbow, measured as the lean of your upper arm off vertical, allowed from 5° behind to 15° in front; and how far your hip sits off the straight line from shoulder to ankle, allowed ±5°. Above the line your hips are piked and are told to come down, below it they are sagging and are told to lift.

    Knee raise — standing side on, tall, one knee up: the angle at that knee between hip and ankle, and the angle at its ankle between knee and toe, both a right angle with 5° either way. Hold ten seconds, lower slowly, and the rep counts when you are back to standing. Ten reps. How far the thigh has to come up to count as raised is a setting, not a mark — it is what tells a raise from standing still, so that the two right angles are the only things being judged.

    What gets said first — the fault earliest in the chain of cause, not the one furthest out. For the wall sit that is feet, then knee, then back; for the plank, shoulders, then hips. Where your feet or your elbows are decides what the rest of you can do, so a correction further along the chain asks for something the base will not give.

    The set — for the holds, sixty seconds counted down from the moment the position is right; for the knee raise, ten seconds a rep and ten reps. Coming out of it pauses the clock rather than running it down, so sixty seconds means sixty seconds of the exercise. The time is called at 45, 30, 10 and 5 seconds left.

    The camera cannot see your spine rounding between hip and shoulder; no pose model gives a mid-spine point, so both moves judge that line by its ends and nothing more. Everything runs on this device. The camera image is never uploaded, and the recording you download is made here in the browser.
