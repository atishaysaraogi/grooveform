      The numbers

      Wall sit — the angle at your knee between hip and ankle, which should sit in 85–110°; the angle your shin makes with the floor, plumb at 90° and allowed 85–95°, where past 95° your heels are ahead of your knees and under 85° they are behind them; and how far the line from your hip to your shoulder leans off vertical, allowed ±12°.

      Elbow plank — where your shoulder sits over your elbow, measured as the lean of your upper arm off vertical, allowed from 5° behind to 15° in front; and how far your hip sits off the straight line from shoulder to ankle, allowed ±5°. Above the line your hips are piked and are told to come down, below it they are sagging and are told to lift.

      Knee raise — standing side on, tall, one knee up: the angle at that knee between hip and ankle, and the angle at its heel between the toe and the knee, the first allowed 80–100° and the second 60–100°. Hold ten seconds, lower slowly, and the rep counts when you are back to standing.

      Glute bridge — lying on your back side on, knees bent, feet flat, phone on its side: the shin's angle at the heel between toe and knee, allowed 85–110°, which is where your feet are; the angle at the hip between knee and shoulder, at least 160° at the top; how far the hip rises above the knee, at most 3°; and the foot's line off the floor, ±10° — checked first, then the shin, then the hip. Hold two seconds at the top, lower slowly, and the rep counts when the hips are back down.

      Donkey kick — on hands and knees, side on: the arms plumb from the floor, 85–105°, elbows straight (at least 165°); the back level, hip to shoulder within ±10° of the floor; the working knee at a right angle, 80–100°; and the lift, the angle at the hip between knee and shoulder, at least 160° at the top and no more than 5° above the line. Hold two seconds at the top, lower slowly, and the rep counts when the knee is back down. One leg per set, the sets alternating.
