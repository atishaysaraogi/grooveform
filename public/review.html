An exercise is one file. Start from a pose, draw the movement, click the figure to say what is measured, and the file writes itself: the phone, the words, the skeleton, the numbers all come with defaults you can change. When it is whole it stands in the library on this browser, the Recordings tab judges videos with it and Try it live runs it in the coach. Download it and drop it into public/exercises.

Drag a joint: the bone above it turns and everything below comes round with it, every limb keeping its length. The hip drags the whole body. The other keyframe sits behind, faint. In a side view the far limbs are the dimmer handles.

      Rep by rep

      Each rep the coach saw — counted, or an attempt it dropped — with every fault it had on the screen inside it and from when to when, whether the fault was said and at what moment, and the set-up faults in the pause before it. A fault has to hold for the persist time to be listed, as it has to before it is said; a flicker shorter than that is left out. Move a number above and this changes with it. Tap a rep to go to it.
