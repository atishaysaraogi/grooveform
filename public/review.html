Back to the coach

An exercise is one file. Start from a pose, draw the movement, add the measurements — a kind and its points, from a list or a tap on the figure — and the file writes itself: the phone, the words, the skeleton, the numbers all come with defaults you can change. When it is whole it stands in the library on this browser, the Recordings tab judges videos with it and Try it live runs it in the coach. Download it and drop it into public/exercises.

From the camera, drag a joint: the bone above it turns and everything below comes round with it, every limb keeping its length. The hip drags the whole body. The other keyframe sits behind, faint. In a side view the far limbs are the dimmer handles. From above, the camera sits at the bottom of the picture: drag a joint down to bring it toward the camera, up to take it away, and what hangs off it comes along; a limb that comes toward the camera or goes away from it can only be shown here. Isometric is a look at both together, not editable.

      Rep by rep

      Each rep the coach saw — counted, or an attempt it dropped — with why it counted or did not (how far it got against the lines, how long the hold clock ran against the hold asked, which band broke the position), then every fault it had on the screen inside it and from when to when, whether the fault was said and at what moment, and the set-up faults in the pause before it. A fault has to hold for the persist time to be listed, as it has to before it is said; a flicker shorter than that is left out. Move a number above and this changes with it. Tap a rep to go to it.
